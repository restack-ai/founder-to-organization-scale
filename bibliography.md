## Caveats

Public company operating manuals and blog posts are excellent for mechanisms, but weak for strict causal inference. They show what scaled companies publicly document, not controlled proof that a single practice caused scale.

AI-agent organizational impact remains early. Some evidence comes from vendor documentation and workforce surveys. Treat those as directional signals, not settled organizational science.
