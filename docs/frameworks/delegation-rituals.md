# Delegation Rituals

Delegation is not an event. It is a recurring operating practice.

## Weekly: Founder Decision Review

Purpose: identify decisions still flowing to the founder that should move down.

Questions:

- Which decisions did the founder make this week?
- Which were truly founder-level?
- Which were habit, fear, or lack of role clarity?
- What guardrail would let the next similar decision move down?

## Weekly: Team Decision Log Review

Purpose: reduce hidden approval loops.

Questions:

- Which decisions are open?
- Which decisions are waiting on founder input?
- Which decisions have no owner?
- Which decisions have reopened due to poor context?

## Monthly: Delegation Audit

Track:

- number of founder approvals;
- number of DRI-owned decisions;
- median decision latency;
- escalation rate;
- decision reopen rate;
- AI-agent actions executed without manual approval by risk tier.

## Quarterly: Operating System Retro

Discuss:

- Are decision rights still correct?
- Are people afraid to decide?
- Are we over-documenting?
- Are AI agents increasing throughput or adding review burden?
- What must remain centralized?

## Ritual design principle

Good rituals create a system where the founder can safely let go without the organization drifting into chaos.
