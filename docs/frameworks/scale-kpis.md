# Scale KPIs

Measure whether decision-making is actually scaling.

| KPI | Definition | Desired direction | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founder decision load | Share of tracked decisions requiring founder final approval | Down | Direct measure of de-bottlenecking |
| Median decision latency | Time from proposal opened to final decision | Down | Shows whether queues are shrinking |
| DRI coverage | % of active projects with one named owner | Up to near 100% | Prevents committee ownership |
| Decision reopen rate | % of decisions reopened within 30–60 days for role / clarity reasons | Down | Measures decision quality and clarity |
| Escalation rate | % of team-level decisions escalated above intended level | Down | Shows guardrails are working |
| Time to first independent decision | Days until a new hire owns a bounded decision | Down | Measures onboarding quality |
| Cross-functional cycle time | Proposal-to-execution time on initiatives involving 2+ functions | Down | Reveals coordination friction |
| Team health score | Red / yellow / green drift across clarity, trust, pace, and alignment | More green | Early warning before attrition or slowdown |
| Autonomy pulse | Employee feedback on autonomy, clarity, psychological safety | Up | Important for retention and ownership culture |
| Agent approval burden | % of agent-prepared work requiring human rework or escalation | Down | Reveals whether agents are adding leverage or noise |
| Agent incident rate | Incidents per agent action by risk tier | Down | Measures governance maturity |

## Minimum viable dashboard

Start with only five metrics:

1. Founder decision load
2. Median decision latency
3. DRI coverage
4. Escalation rate
5. Decision reopen rate

Then add AI-agent metrics once agents are executing meaningful workflows.

## Warning

Do not turn ownership into bureaucracy. The point is not to track every tiny choice. Track decisions that affect strategy, roadmap, customer commitments, architecture, people, money, or risk.
