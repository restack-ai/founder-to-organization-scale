# Case Study: Intercom

Intercom is useful because it describes the practical middle-stage startup problem: as teams grow, informal ownership stops working.

## Relevant examples

- Product managers should not build the roadmap alone.
- Product teams should participate in roadmap construction.
- Support teams need Areas of Responsibility as they scale.

## Why it matters

Intercom's writing captures a common transition:

```text
Small team: everyone does everything
Growing team: everyone doing everything creates confusion
Scaled team: domains need explicit owners
```

In product work, top-down roadmaps can create low ownership and a gap between strategy and execution. Intercom's solution was to give teams more access to customer input, metrics, company strategy, and roadmap construction.

In support work, Areas of Responsibility helped avoid the "too many cooks in some areas and neglect in others" problem.

## Startup lesson

Ownership must become explicit before the organization becomes large enough for ambiguity to become expensive.

## Repo application

Use the Intercom pattern for:

- roadmap delegation;
- support ownership;
- domain ownership;
- Areas of Responsibility.
