# Case Study: Atlassian

Atlassian is a strong example of decision-role clarity and team-health instrumentation.

## Relevant practices

- DACI decision framework
- Team Health Monitor
- Open company values
- Structured team discussions
- One clear approver for cross-functional decisions

## Why it matters

Atlassian's tools are practical because they address a real scaling problem: cross-functional ambiguity.

DACI works because it separates:

- Driver: the person moving the decision forward;
- Approver: the one person who makes the call;
- Contributors: people who provide input;
- Informed: people who need to know.

The Health Monitor matters because distributed decision-making fails when teams are unclear, blocked, or misaligned but nobody surfaces the issue early.

## Startup lesson

Use lightweight structure before friction becomes politics.

## Repo application

Use the Atlassian pattern for:

- cross-functional decisions;
- team health reviews;
- role clarity rituals;
- decision retrospectives.
